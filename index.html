A few useful answers

Before we begin.

Can we start with just an idea?

Absolutely. A goal, a reference or a rough sketch is enough to start the conversation. We’ll work out the direction and scope together.

What’s included in a project?

Deliverables, formats, source files and revision rounds are defined in the proposal before production starts. Nothing is left to assumption.

How do payments and revisions work?

The project starts with a 50% deposit. The remaining 50% is due after approval, before final delivery. Revision rounds are agreed in the project scope.

Can you work with our existing team?

Yes. I can take ownership of a defined workstream or collaborate within your design and production pipeline, with agreed milestones and handoffs.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Project updates and feedback can be asynchronous, with review calls when a live conversation makes a decision easier.
